Game Rules & Instructions

1. Board Setup: 100 tiles containing hidden ladders and snakes.

2. Rolling the Dice: Teams take turns rolling the 6-sided dice to move forward.

3. Math Challenges: Land on tiles to solve Form 1 KSSM DLP questions.
• Ladder Tile: Correct answer = Climb up! Wrong answer = Stay at bottom.
• Snake Head: Correct answer = Defend & dodge slide! Wrong answer = Slide down snake.
• Standard Tile: Correct answer = Keep position! Wrong answer = Step back 2 spaces.

4. Winning the Game: First team/player to reach Tile 100 wins the game!
